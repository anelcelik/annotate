## Screen Annotator Pro 5.6.0 — more shapes, stamps, save your marks, hold to draw, countdown

### New
- **Filled shapes.** Rectangle and Circle have a **FILL** choice on the dock:
  - *Off*: outline only, as before.
  - *Tint*: a light wash of colour inside the outline.
  - *Solid*: filled in. Lower the opacity to make it see-through.
- **Double and curved arrows.** The Arrow has a **HEADS** choice, *One* or *Both*. To curve an arrow, select it (**V**) and drag the new handle in its middle; the head follows the curve.
- **Speech bubbles.** The Text tool's **BOX** choice is now *Off*, *Box* or *Bubble*. A bubble points at something: select it and drag the tip of its tail wherever you want.
- **Stamps (G).** Click to place a ✓, ✗, !, ? or ★ in any colour, sized with SIZE. Pick which one on the dock's **STAMP** choice.
- **Save your marks and open them again.** **Ctrl+S** saves everything on screen to a `.samarks` file, and **Ctrl+O** opens one. Both are also in the tray menu. This is useful for a slide you mark up the same way every week, or to pick up where you left off.
  - Opened marks go on top of what's already there, and one **Ctrl+Z** removes them all again.
  - Blur and pixelate keep hiding what they covered.
- **Hold a key to draw.** Settings → Shortcuts → *Hold a key to draw*: pick **Right Ctrl** or **Right Shift**.
  - Hold it and you're drawing. Let go and your clicks go to the app underneath again, and that app gets the keyboard back.
  - This is the quickest way to add a mark in the middle of a demo.
- **3-2-1 before recording.** A countdown in the middle of the recorded area gives you three seconds to get ready. It never appears in the video, and pressing Record again during the countdown cancels it. Turn it off in Settings → Recording.

### Changed
- The Zoom shortcut in Settings is now called *Magnifier shortcut*, to match 5.5.1's magnifier.

### Which file do I want?
| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ScreenAnnotatorPro-Setup.msi` | Classic installer: Program Files, Start menu, optional desktop shortcut. |
| `ScreenAnnotatorPro-Portable.exe` | One file, no install. |
| `ScreenAnnotatorProVideo.msix` + `.cer` | Sideload test build of the Store package — see `INSTALL-MSIX.txt`. Conflicts with a Store install. |
| `ScreenAnnotatorPro-StoreSubmission.msix` | Unsigned, for Partner Center only — not for installing. |

The .exe and .msi builds are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen asks once: *More info → Run anyway*.
