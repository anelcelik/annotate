### Description

**Draw on your screen, blur what's private, and record it all — right on top of any app.**

Screen Annotator Pro puts a transparent drawing layer over your whole desktop. Circle a button, drop numbered steps, blur an email address before anyone sees it, point with a laser dot — then hit Record and every mark goes into the video at full resolution. No second app, no editing afterwards: stop, and the MP4 is already on disk, with one-click export to GIF or WebM.

Click-through mode keeps your marks on screen while your mouse goes back to the app underneath, so you can keep demoing, typing and switching windows without the overlay getting in the way.

Made for presentations, remote meetings, tutorials, teaching, support calls and bug reports. Works across all your monitors, at every display scaling from 100 % to 400 %. No account, no cloud, no upload — everything stays on your PC.

## Description (up to 5.0, for reference)

**Draw, highlight, and annotate anything on your screen — in real time, without interrupting what's behind it.**

Screen Annotator Pro places a fully transparent overlay over your entire desktop. Pick up any tool and start drawing instantly. When you're done, everything disappears with a single click — no trace left on your actual screen.

Perfect for presentations, video recordings, remote meetings, tutorials, teaching, and live demos. Whether you need to circle something quickly, walk through numbered steps, blur sensitive information, or point at a detail with a laser dot — Screen Annotator Pro has the right tool for the job.

Works across **all connected monitors** and is fully optimised for **4K, 8K, and all DPI scaling settings** (100%, 125%, 150%, 200%, 400% and every fractional step in between).

## What's New in This Version

**Version 5.1 — Fixes that matter, and a lighter app**

- Annotations now land exactly where you drew them in screenshots and recordings at 125 % and 150 % display scaling
- Recording with the microphone works again, and picks your microphone automatically
- The eraser no longer leaves black streaks in recordings or holes in screenshots
- Blur now fully covers what it hides, right up to the edge
- "Start with Windows" now really starts the app when you sign in
- New default shortcuts that don't clash with your browser: Ctrl+Alt+R to record. If another app already uses a shortcut, the app tells you instead of silently ignoring it
- Clear all, deleting a shape and moving a shape can all be undone — and a deleted recording goes to the Recycle Bin
- The app asks before exiting when you'd lose your marks, and finishes saving a running recording first
- Numbered callouts keep counting correctly after an undo
- Dark mode now applies to every window
- Smaller download and lower memory use when the overlay is idle


**Version 4.0 — Redesigned toolbar, dark mode**

- **New horizontal toolbar** — a compact bar docked at the bottom of your screen, replacing the old tall side panel; it shows only the controls the current tool actually uses
- **Dark mode** — switch between Light and Dark in Settings, applied instantly
- **Collapsible toolbar** — double-click to shrink it down to a single draggable icon when you need it out of the way, and expand it back with one click
- **Toolbar remembers its place** — wherever you move it, or collapse it to, it stays there next time you open the app
- **More reliable OCR & Translate** — text recognition now starts loading as soon as you pick the tool instead of after you've made your selection, and translation failures show a clear message instead of an error page
- **Multi-monitor fixes** — Settings, Help, and Capture windows, and Snip & Read's on-screen capture, are now positioned correctly across every monitor arrangement

**Version 3.0.1 — Bug fix**
- **Bug fix for some users app will not start due to missing dlls**

**Version 2.8 — Major feature update**
- **OCR & Translate** — Added on screen Translation and Text recognition

- **Shift-lock drawing** — hold Shift to snap lines and arrows to exact 45° angles, and rectangles to perfect squares
- **Full multi-monitor support** — the overlay now spans every connected display automatically
- **HiDPI / 4K / 8K certified** — Per-Monitor V2 DPI awareness, crisp at every scale factor
