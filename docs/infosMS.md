# Microsoft Store Listing — Screen Annotator Pro

---

## Description

**Draw, highlight, and annotate anything on your screen — in real time, without interrupting what's behind it.**

Screen Annotator Pro places a fully transparent overlay over your entire desktop. Pick up any tool and start drawing instantly. When you're done, everything disappears with a single click — no trace left on your actual screen.

Perfect for presentations, video recordings, remote meetings, tutorials, teaching, and live demos. Whether you need to circle something quickly, walk through numbered steps, blur sensitive information, or point at a detail with a laser dot — Screen Annotator Pro has the right tool for the job.

Works across **all connected monitors** and is fully optimised for **4K, 8K, and all DPI scaling settings** (100%, 125%, 150%, 200%, 400% and every fractional step in between).

---

## What's New in This Version

**Version 4.0 — Redesigned toolbar, dark mode**

- **New horizontal dock toolbar** — replaces the old 940px vertical panel with a compact bar docked at the bottom of the screen, showing only the properties the active tool actually uses
- **Dark mode** — a Light/Dark switch in Settings, applied instantly, remembered next launch
- **Collapsible tool puck** — double-click the dock's grip to shrink it down to a single draggable icon, click to expand back
- **Drag position is remembered** — moving the dock (or the puck) no longer resets when you switch tools
- **Redesigned Settings, Help, Capture, and OCR windows** — flat, consistent styling to match the new dock, replacing the old dark rounded popups
- **Multi-monitor fixes** — Settings/Help/Capture no longer land in a gap between monitors on non-simple layouts; Snip & Read (OCR) now captures the correct region regardless of monitor arrangement
- **Translate reliability** — a blocked/rate-limited translation request now shows a clear message instead of a raw error page

## Product Features

Draw, highlight, blur, annotate, and laser-point anything on your screen in real time — with full undo, multi-monitor support, and 4K / 8K HiDPI scaling built in.
